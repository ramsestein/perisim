PERISIM post-hoc drift: missing values

Exploratory · not pre-registered · aggregates only

Exploratory, post hoc. Added on 2026-10-28 at the lead's request, after the drift analysis; not pre-registered, no decision rule. The frozen INSPIRE metric with each site's own case base (as in the drift analysis, same test set, labels and k); only the points of the missing-data values move, all at once: the 'missing' value of every continuous variable and missingness indicator (it starts at the median's point, as INSPIRE imputes the median) and the 'unobserved' value of every binary. Every other point is frozen. The groups: measurement (a value not recorded), structural (hours since a support that was never used) and source (a binary whose source was not recorded, e.g. the medication tables); each group's row takes only its own adapted points (no refit). INSPIRE is the control (in-sample).

Which missing values move

Left: share of the site's stays with the value missing. Right: distance of the missing value's point from the mean of the observed values (in the metric, so it includes the variable's weight); ○ frozen (where INSPIRE puts it), ● adapted to the site. Moving away = the site wants 'missing' to mean something of its own. Values with < 10 stays at a site are not shown. The medication flags share one 'unobserved' pattern (the medication tables of the stay) and are summarised by their largest move.
Move: distance between the frozen and the adapted point. Half cosine: agreement in direction of the moves fitted on two patient halves (1 = the same direction). Weight: effective weight of the whole variable at the site (% of Σ w).
