Exploratory · not pre-registered · aggregates only

Exploratory, post hoc. Added on 2026-10-28 after the external validation and the transport analyses had been seen; not part of the pre-registration (SPECS §6.2b, amendment of 2026-10-24) and no decision rule. The frozen artefacts were only read (SHA-256 identical at the start and the end); the split, labels (cross-fitted tertiles) and k are those of the transport stage. LightGBM and the logistic regression are nested as in the CV. Recalibration: multinomial, p'c ∝ exp(ac + b·log pc), on the nested local tuning subsets of analysis D, Gaussian prior sd 2 on a and log b. 95 % CIs: patient bootstrap, the same resamples for every method (paired). Shapes: the per-site consensus of analysis E on INSPIRE's grid, each site divided by its scale.

Where the sites differ: distribution vs relationship

Two different things make a variable behave differently at another site. A different population (covariate shift): the site's stays fall in other parts of INSPIRE's grid — distribution shift (continuous: mean displacement in INSPIRE percentiles, Wasserstein-1 between the site's and INSPIRE's own percentiles, with its sign: + = higher values; nominal: share of stays in other categories; the PSI over INSPIRE's bins is in the CSV, but a coarser recording resolution inflates it), stays beyond INSPIRE's range (where every gj is flat) and missingness. A different relationship: the shape gj re-estimated at the site (analysis E, divided by the site scale) departs from INSPIRE's. The weight wj says how much the variable counts in the metric.
