# Feasibility: hypotension in the next 6 h, ICU after surgery

Original tables, every postoperative adult ICU stay (the first of each hospitalization, ICU deaths included), first 72 h. Landmarks every 2 h from 2 h to 48 h after the ICU admission, in the ICU and alive, with a MAP in the last hour. Hypotension: an hour whose median MAP is < 65 mmHg (death in the window counts as positive). Shares; counts ≥ 10.
